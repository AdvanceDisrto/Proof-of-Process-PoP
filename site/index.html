ΔiΔ / EXECUTION EVIDENCE

A signature is evidence.
Not a universal truth claim.

The backend contract fails closed on absent identity, signing keys or evidence. The browser demo below generates a local SHA-256 digest—not a signed receipt or independent verification.

Verification boundaries

  • No evidence → no verified claim
  • No trusted signer → no signed attestation
  • Missing dependency → no DAG reconciliation
  • No verified authority → no certification

Trust Engine / Z3, hardware PUF, energy metering and arbitration shown in the concept art are not represented as production-attested capabilities here.
