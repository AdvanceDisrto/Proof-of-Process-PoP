# iNNOVULIS — Corporate Manifest & Operating Charter
**Draft 0.1 | 27 October 2026 | Not filed, adopted, incorporated, or legally binding**

## Identity and purpose

**iNNOVULIS** is the proposed commercial identity for an evidence-bound verification and receipt technology business. Its central product is **the receipt**: a independently-checkable record binding declared intent, observed processing, authorized keys, evidence commitments, and the limits of what has actually been verified. Technology should serve humanity.

**Mission:** Make the path from a claim to its evidence inspectable, portable, falsifiable and fair. Make unsupported assertions visibly distinguishable from verified, externally corroborated events. Build interoperability across software, hardware, enterprise infrastructure, and the physical world without misleading users about certainty.

**Product promises.** The platform may authenticate specific digitally signed statements, make local histories tamper-evident, preserve rejected claims and correction lineage, and expose independently reproducible verification. It does **not** promise infallible people, sensors, electronics, AI, human decisions or laws of physics. The proper goal is observable limits and corrective accountability, not mathematically guaranteed truth.

## Articles of governance

**Article I — Receipt primacy.** No event may advance its evidence tier solely because a UI says VERIFIED, an AI generated a narrative, a signature exists, or a transaction is on a blockchain.

**Article II — Separation of roles.** Issuer, witness, reviewer, verifier, and beneficiary must be distinguished. Independence is evaluated by actual control and trust anchors, not merely distinct key identifiers.

**Article III — Fail-closed, with recourse.** Invalid signatures, lost provenance, conflicts, expired credentials, key revocations, clock uncertainty, unverifiable devices and unavailable evidence result in a limited or rejected status. There is a path for humans to contest false negatives and incorrect claims.

**Article IV — Failure preservation.** Original evidence and prior errors are not rewritten to hide faults. Fixes create new linked receipts; supersession does not imply deletion.

**Article V — No self-certification of physical reality.** Hardware evidence is a claim attributed to a configured sensor and device identity. Corroboration depends on measured device security, calibration, provenance, inspection and validation. A PUF or digital signature does not inherently make its claims true.

**Article VI — Human accountability.** The system never determines criminal guilt, prosecutions, adjudication, housing rights, or legal liability. Thresholds from AI heuristics are not judicial findings. Financial settlement and physical actuation require separately authorized legal and technical controls.

**Article VII — Data stewardship.** Minimize collection; separate public commitments from personal data; encrypt sensitive storage; support appropriate retention, consent, access controls and correction mechanisms. Never expose identities, medical records or private arbitration materials to public chains by default.

**Article VIII — Cryptographic agility.** Algorithms, trust registries, devices, certificates, and security thresholds are versioned and replaceable. Key compromise triggers revocation and remediation. No cryptographic primitive guarantees permanence or perfect resistance to all future attacks.

**Article IX — Transparency of maturity.** Every release distinguishes `CONCEPT`, `SIMULATED`, `COMPILED`, `TESTED`, `INTEGRATED`, `EXTERNALLY_ATTESTED`, and `PRODUCTION` states. No unobserved test is marked passed. No fabricated telemetry appears in a production metric feed.

**Article X — Independent governance.** Significant policy changes and releases should require at least two truly independent approvals where practicable, logged in the chain. Threshold signatures reduce key concentration but do not make persons or institutions infallible.

**Article XI — Open verification, controlled rights.** Publish auditable interface contracts and verification procedures while maintaining lawful access controls, privacy, trade secrets and intended IP licensing. Patentability and trademark ownership depend on separate searches, filings and counsel.

**Article XII — Production safety.** No funds, public disbursements, emergency actions, hardware engravers or government systems are activated merely by passing software unit tests. Production requires independent testing, threat modeling, regulatory review where relevant, and explicit deployment approval.

## Proposed business lines

1. **iNNOVULIS Receipt SaaS:** Receipt APIs, pinned issuer registries, independently reproducible verification, audit export, policy engines and human review.
2. **iNNOVULIS Material / Alpha-1:** Prototyping of secure-element-backed receipt capture and tamper-evident physical media, with controlled real-world tests.
3. **iNNOVULIS Verification SDK:** Offline browser verifier, CLI verifier, service integrations, signed source registry and supported formats.
4. **iNNOVULIS Enterprise Integrations:** Proof-of-process for legitimate invoices, software deployments, equipment service records and supply-chain custody, subject to source consent and contract authorization.
